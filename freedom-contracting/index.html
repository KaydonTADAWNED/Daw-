Straight answers.

Will you actually show up? +

Yes. You get a start date before we begin, and if anything changes, we call you. You will not be left wondering where the crew is.

What if the price changes? +

Your price is in writing before work starts. If you ask for something new, or we find a problem behind a wall, we show you and agree on it with you before we do it.

Do you work on rentals and apartments? +

Yes. We turn units for landlords and property owners, from floors and paint to full kitchens and baths, and whole building exteriors.

Who do I talk to during the job? +

One person, from the first walk-through to the final one. You always have a name and a number.
